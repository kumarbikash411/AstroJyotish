# AstroJyotish Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 22, 2026**

AstroJyotish ("the app") is provided by **AstroJyotish**
("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app
uses, how it is handled, and the choices available to you.

For privacy questions, contact us at **kumar.bikash415@gmail.com**.

## Information you enter

The app lets you enter a name, date of birth, time of birth, birthplace, and
questions for its astrology guidance features.

- Birth profile details, calculated chart information, questions, and sample
  answers are held in the app's memory while it is running. The app does not
  send these details or questions to an AstroJyotish server. The profile and
  question history are not saved by the app between sessions.
- Birthplace text is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find matching
  cities and their coordinates and time zones. The app uses the returned
  location on the device to calculate charts and local timings. Open-Meteo
  receives the search query and may process it under its own policies. See
  [Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).
- Questions receive sample responses generated in the app. They are not sent
  to an AI service or to an AstroJyotish server.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in questions.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected display language locally on your device so it can
remember that choice. It does not use this storage to save your birth profile
or questions. You can remove locally stored app data using your device's app
storage settings or by uninstalling the app; uninstalling or clearing app data
also removes the saved language choice.

## Purchases and subscriptions

If subscription purchases are enabled in your version of the app, purchases and
restores are handled through Google Play and RevenueCat. Those providers may
process purchase, subscription, transaction, app-instance, and device-related
information needed to complete purchases, prevent fraud, restore purchases,
and verify access. We receive subscription status and entitlement information
needed to provide paid app features; we do not receive your full payment-card
details. Google Play and RevenueCat handle data under their own privacy
policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

Subscriptions are managed through the store account used to purchase them.
Check the store's purchase screen for the price, renewal period, and applicable
terms.

## Device and service information

When you use the app's birthplace search or, where enabled, purchase features,
your device communicates directly with the relevant third-party service. Those
services may receive technical information such as an IP address, request
metadata, or device/app identifiers as part of normal network and billing
operations. We do not operate an AstroJyotish backend for profile, chart, or
question data.

The app does not include advertising. RevenueCat may process purchase events
and related technical information as part of its subscription service when
billing is enabled.

## How information is used

Information is used only to:

- calculate astrology charts and Panchang timings on your device;
- look up a birthplace when you request it;
- remember your language preference;
- process or restore a subscription, if subscription purchases are enabled;
- respond to privacy or support messages you choose to send us; and
- operate and maintain the app.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share profile details,
calculated charts, or question text with an AstroJyotish server. The birthplace
search and optional purchase flows share the limited information described
above with Open-Meteo, Google Play, and RevenueCat, respectively. Those
providers may also disclose information as described in their own policies or
where legally required.

## Retention and deletion

Birth profile details, chart calculations, questions, and sample answers are
kept in app memory only and are cleared when the app process ends. The selected
language remains in local app storage until you change it, clear app data, or
uninstall the app. Purchase and transaction records are retained by Google Play
and RevenueCat according to their policies and operational or legal
requirements. Contact the relevant provider about records they control.

## Security

Profile and question data are processed locally by the app and are not uploaded
to an AstroJyotish server. Birthplace lookups and billing communications use
the third-party services described above. No method of storage or transmission
is completely secure, and third-party services are governed by their own
security practices.

## Children's privacy

AstroJyotish is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from
children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information
through a third-party service should contact that service and contact us using
the details below.

## Your choices and rights

You may choose not to enter profile details or use birthplace search. You can
change or clear the language preference through your device's app storage
settings. You may manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play. Depending
on where you live, you may have privacy rights concerning information handled
by third-party providers; contact those providers directly for requests about
their records.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change.
The updated version will be published at the same policy URL with a revised
effective date.
